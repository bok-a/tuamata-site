Practice — Environmental Policy

Nature-Based Assets. Long-Term Ecological Returns.

We develop and steward reforestation and ecological restoration projects aligned to national carbon credit schemes and international biodiversity frameworks. We work directly with landowners, iwi (tribal) stakeholders, and policy architects to design climate-positive projects that meet investor-grade due diligence standards. Our methodology includes remote sensing, ecological baselining, and carbon yield modeling to quantify natural capital.

Methodology

How we quantify natural capital.

Remote Sensing

Satellite and aerial data tracking canopy, land-use change, and project extent over time.

Ecological Baselining

Establishing the pre-project ecological baseline a restoration is measured against.

Carbon Yield Modeling

Projected carbon yield modeled to investor-grade due diligence standards.
